# CASA Förderverein website brief

## Authorization and context

The user approved the modern, light Zwischenraum logo and the expanded branding
kit in chat `01a1891b-9be8-7d30-abe8-aca0b9989b28`. They then requested a new Codex
project using the CASA tech stack and transfer of this chat into that project.
This repository is the new project. The old Vite site at
`/Users/rahmanshafiee/Documents/CASA Verein` remains untouched.

## Art direction

Visual thesis: a quiet editorial identity, open curves and careful whitespace,
charcoal type on warm white, cyan for connection and a small yellow focal point.
The first viewport should feel like a typographic cultural poster.

Content plan: hero, association mission, access to education, planned programmes,
meeting at the school, independent learning and coaching, downloadable overview.
Use the user's copy in `source-copy.md`. Use a typographic hero and the approved
graphic system; do not dilute it with generic charity photography or icons.

Interaction thesis: draw the hero curves on entry, drift the artwork slightly
with scroll where supported, reveal sections gently, underline real actions.
Remove decorative motion for reduced-motion preferences. Content must remain
visible if JavaScript is disabled.

## Content boundaries

- B1 preparation and scholarships are proposals under development.
- The school, not the association, has the supplied 40-year/80-nation context.
- No invented donation workflow, IBAN, contact person, board or register number.
- No newsletter, analytics, tracking, third-party fonts, registration or fake form.
- Local-only partnership enquiries were authorized on 2026-10-30. The popup saves
  contact and meeting preferences privately on this laptop, with clear preview
  messaging. It does not send mail, book appointments, or accept payments.
- The information-card download is a real supplied PDF.
